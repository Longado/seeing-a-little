The source images are not altered on disk. The room, snow portal, pipe, dialogue, animated portrait effects, bridge, and surrounding distortion are original interpretive work for this prototype. The self-portrait is the 1887 Chicago painting, not an archival reconstruction of a specific pipe portrait. The added pipe is an intentional game prop. Dialogue is fictional, not quoted historical speech. The bedroom scene interprets forms and colors rather than reconstructing historically measured dimensions. Textures on modeled furniture and walls are procedurally drawn brush marks.
